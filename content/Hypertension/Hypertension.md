# Categories of Hypertension

- Normal BP
- Elevated BP
	- SBP 120-130 *and* DBP < 80
- Stage 1 HTN
	- SBP 130-139 *or* DBP 80-89
- Stage 2 HTN
	- SBP ≥ 140 *or* DBP ≥ 90

# Measuring Blood Pressure

- Out-of-office BP is superior to office BP for predicting target organ damage or major adverse events [^1]
- Get an accurate device: validatebp.org
- Home BP monitoring *preferred*[^2]
	- Seated
	- Ideally in the morning before breakfast and at night before going to bed.
	- Rest for ~5 mins before first measurement
	- Recommended to do for *at least* 3, but ideally 7 days

# Secondary Causes of Hypertension

- Physical exam
	- [[Aortic Coarctation|Coarctation of the Aorta]]: assessment of a radial-femoral delay, upper and lower extremity blood pressures, and auscultation for murmurs of both the anterior and posterior chest for evidence of collateral circulation should the coarctation be severe.
- Renal artery duplex ultrasound can be used to diagnose [[Renal Artery Stenosis|renal artery stenosis]] or [[Fibromuscular Dysplasia|fibromuscular dysplasia]].
- [[Hyperaldosteronism]]
- TSH
	- hyperthyroidism > hypOthyroidism can cause systemic HTN

# Trials

## SPRINT Trial

- Importantly excluded those with [[Diabetes]] and [[Stroke]]

## STEP Trial

- Compared intensive (SBP 110-130 mmHg) versus standard SBP targets (130-150) in older adults (60-80 yo).
- Demonstrated that lowering SBP to a tighter goal significantly reduces MACE.
- Primarily utilized regimens including olmesartan, amlodipine, and HCTZ

## QUARTET and QUARTET-USA Trials

- Compared a single daily pill containing quarter-standard doses of four different blood pressure medications (a "quadpill") versus standard-dose monotherapy
