- Cardiogenic Shock includes decompensated HF shock and MI-shock.
	- HF-shock appears to derive more benefit from [[Intra-aortic Balloon Pump (IABP)|IABP]] than MI-shock
- CS is a common diagnosis among patients admitted to contemporary CICUs, prevalent in approximately 15% of total CICU admissions. [^berg-cqo]

# Management

## [[Pulmonary Artery Catheter (PAC)]]

- Hemodynamic tailoring with diuretics, vasoactive medications, and mechanical circulatory support (MCS) should be guided by both objective measures from the PAC, including a CVP target between 5-10 mm Hg, pulmonary artery wedge pressure generally ≤15 mm Hg, and cardiac index ≥2.2 L/min/m2, as well as markers of appropriate perfusion, such as lactate, mental status, and urine output.
