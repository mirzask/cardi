- Auscultation 👂: Continuous "machine like" murmur
	- **continuous** murmur because continuous shunting as the aortic pressure is higher than the pulmonary artery pressure in both systole and diastole
- Physiologically similar to [[Ventricular Septal Defect (VSD)|VSD]], both are post-tricuspid shunts
- Complications:
	- Left heart enlargement
	- Pulmonary vascular remodeling
	- Infective endarteritis (although not indication for SBE prophylaxis)

# Echo

In (A) you see flow going from the proximal descending aorta through the PDA into the proximal left pulmonary artery. In (B) we see [[Echo Doppler|spectral doppler]] showing **continuous flow** with higher velocities in systole than in diastole.
