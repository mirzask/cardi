# Medication Management

- [[Sodium-glucose cotransporter 2 inhibitors (SGLT2i)|SGLT2i]] should be discontinued **3 to 4 days** before surgery to minimize the risk of perioperative ketoacidosis associated with their use.

# Myocardial Injury after Non-cardiac surgery (MINS)

- ⚠️ Do NOT ignore b/c it portends real consequences for affected patients

# Peri-operative [[Atrial Fibrillation (AFib)|AFib]]

- These patients should be followed closely after surgery to treat reversible causes of arrhythmia and to assess the need for rhythm control and long-term anticoagulation.
