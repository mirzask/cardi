
# Work-up for etiology of ischemic stroke or TIA

- Parenchymal imaging (MRI/CT)
	- Changed suspected localization and etiological mechanism in over 1/3 of patients
- Arterial status
	- MRA/CTA/Carotid/Transcranial Ultrasound
- Cardiac Workup
	- ECG/telemetry ([[Ambulatory ECG Monitoring|Ambulatory rhythm monitoring]] as outpatient)
	- Echocardiogram (TTE/TEE)
- Risk factor assessment
	- [[Hypertension]]
	- [[Hyperlipidemia|Lipid Management]]
	- HbA1C
