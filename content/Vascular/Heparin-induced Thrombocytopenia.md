# 4T Score

- Recall, for thrombosis the event can be arterial (e.g. MI) or venous (e.g. DVT)
- Interpretation
	- 0-3 (low risk) → HIT unlikely → monitor platelets
	- 4-5 (moderate risk) → HIT possible → stop 🚫 heparin
	- ≥6 (high risk) → HIT likely → stop 🚫 heparin

# Treatment

- Stop all heparin
- Obtain platelet factor 4 antibody testing (or SRA for <u>confirmation</u>)
	- 📝 Sometimes SRA can take ~1 week to get result. PF4 Ab results *faster*.
- Start direct thrombin inhibitor
	- Argatroban (⚠️ hepatic excretion)
	- Bivalirudin
	- Warfarin (after platelet count recovery)
	- DOAC
- 📝 Place heparin on allergy list
