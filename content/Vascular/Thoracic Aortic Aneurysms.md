- Use indexed measurements in patients at extremes of height
- Defining “rapid aortic root growth” or “ascending aortic aneurysm growth”
	- Sporadic aneurysms: ≥0.5 cm in 1 year or ≥0.3 cm per year in 2 consecutive years
	- [[Bicuspid Aortic Valve|BAV]] or heritable thoracic aortic diseases: ≥0.3 cm in 1 year
- Screen 1st degree relatives with aortic imaging if:
	- aneurysms of the [[Aortic Root|aortic root]] or ascending aorta
	- [[Aortic Dissection|aortic dissection]]
- Guidelines recommend CT/[[Cardiac MRI|MRI]] measurements of the [[Aortic Root|aortic root]] and ascending aorta be measured from **inner-edge to inner-edge** using gated images.
	- Measurements of aortic diameter should be completed perpendicular to the axis of blood flow
