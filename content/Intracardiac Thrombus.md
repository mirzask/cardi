# LV Apical Thrombus

## Example

- There is a large LV thrombus (20 mm x 15 mm) in the akinetic apex.
- There is a moderate-sized area of transmural [[Gadolinium|late gadolinium enhancement]] of the distal anterior wall and apex consistent with a [[Myocardial Viability|nonviable]] distal LAD territory myocardial infarction.
- The LCx and RCA territory myocardium is free of [[Gadolinium|late gadolinium enhancement]] and considered fully [[Myocardial Viability|viable]].
