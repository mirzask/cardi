- [[Heart Failure with Reduced Ejection Fraction (HFrEF)]]

- [[Right Heart Failure]]

- The 5 "failures" of new-onset HF:
	- **cardiac** failure
		- e.g. [[Coronary Artery Disease (CAD)|CAD]]
	- **ventricular** failure
		- e.g. [[Non-ischemic Cardiomyopathy|NICM]], such as [[Amyloidosis|amyloidosis]], [[Hypertrophic Cardiomyopathy|HCM]]
		- [[Right Heart Failure]]
	- **valve** failure
		- [[0-Valvular Disorders]]
	- **electrical** ⚡ failure
		- e.g. [[Bradycardia|bradycardia]], [[Atrial Fibrillation (AFib)|AFib]], chronic RV pacing
	- **pericardial** failure
		- e.g. [[Constrictive Pericarditis]]
