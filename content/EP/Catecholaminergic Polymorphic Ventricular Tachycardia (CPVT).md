
# Diagnosis
## [[Exercise ECG]]

- Primary method for provoking adrenergically mediated ventricular arrhythmias.[^3]
- Exercise testing typically provokes premature ventricular contractions, followed by the appearance of couplets and non-sustained polymorphic or bidirectional VT in patients with CPVT. [^2]

### Burst Exercise Testing

- Unlike the gradual exercise testing that is typically completed, patients undergo a sudden high workload at the outset of testing. Roston's group "postulate that this simple EST modification better mimics a typical CPVT triggering event and could improve diagnostic sensitivity and therapeutic decision making."[^1]
- [^3] found that "the Burst protocol was associated with more severe ventricular arrhythmias in 20 of 28 patients (71%) and a higher median arrhythmia score than the Bruce protocol (3 vs 1)."
