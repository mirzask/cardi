- Form of [[Atrial Tachycardia#Tachyarrhythmias Enhanced normal automaticity Automatic Atrial Tachycardia|automatic atrial tachycardia]] that is characterized by multiple (≥ 3) P wave morphologies and **irregular** PP intervals
- Thought to be due to *multiple* automatic foci firing at different rates.
- Often seen with acute pulmonary disease
- Unlikely to respond to [[Cardioversion|DCCV]]
- 💊 [[Calcium Channel Blockers (CCBs)]] are typically quite effective for treatment of MAT
