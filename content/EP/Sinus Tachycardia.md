- Causes include [[Pulmonary Embolism|PE]], [[Acute Coronary Syndrome (ACS)|ACS]], hypOthyroidism, [[Ventricular Arrhythmias|ventricular arrhythmias]], and heritable or acquired cardiomyopathic or arrhythmic disorders.
	- Exposure to stimulants (sympathomimetic drugs such as amphetamines) and other medications (such as tricyclic antidepressants) can lead to tachycardia. Abrupt withdrawal of certain medications such as beta-blockers or central sympatholytic agents such as clonidine, may also cause rebound tachycardia.
