# Localization of [[Ventricular Tachycardia|VT]] on [[Electrocardiogram|ECG]]

![[Ventricular Arrhythmias-1746311935044.webp]]

- **Lead V1**
	- Recall, **V1** is located on the **anterior** part of the chest
		- ![[Electrocardiogram _ECG_-1746232364818.webp|329x260]]
	- [[Left Bundle Branch Block|LBBB]]-like → septum (or near the septum)
		- If the <u>terminal</u> part in V1 is <u>predominantly negative</u>, then we call it as left bundle morphology
		- As if the left bundle is blocked, the LV is late, and the activation is going from anterior to posterior.
	- [[Right Bundle Branch Block|RBBB]]-like
		- If the <u>terminal</u> part in V1 is <u>predominantly positive</u>, then we call it as right bundle morphology
