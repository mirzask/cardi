# Management

## Non-pharmacologic management

- Do NOT lie down during the day
- Elevate HOB by 6-9 inches
	- ↓ nocturnal diuresis from the high pressures the kidneys see
- Nighttime meal/sweet snacks
	- Induce postprandial hypOtension

## Pharmacologic management

- 🔑 is timing of daytime pressor agents
	- Avoid pressor agents before bedtime (<4h)
	- Consider short-acting antihypertensive at bedtime
		- Captopril
		- Clonidine
		- Hydralazine
		- Losartan
		- Nitroglycerin patch
