# Modified WHO (mWHO)

- Class 1 (lower risk) to Class 4 (highest risk)
	- **Class I:** No-to-mild increased risk of mortality/morbidity.
	- **Class II/III:** Intermediate/moderate risk, requiring careful individualized evaluation and monitoring.
	- **Class IV:** Extremely high risk of maternal mortality or severe morbidity; pregnancy is generally contraindicated
		- See [[Pre-Conception Counseling]]
