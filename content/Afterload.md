

# [[Right Ventricle|RV]] Afterload

- ⚠️ Additional positive end-expiratory pressure (PEEP) (either due to ventilator support or from obstructive pulmonary disease) will increase RV afterload and can contribute to [[Right Heart Failure|RV failure]]. 
- Although better tolerated *initially*, the RV remains afterload-sensitive with more progressive and chronic changes in load (i.e. chronic [[Pulmonary Hypertension|PH]]).
