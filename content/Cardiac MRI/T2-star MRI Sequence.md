- Most commonly used to detect and quantify iron deposition, e.g. iron overload.
- Quantify iron in the **heart** 🫀 and the **liver**
	- Heart 🫀 (trace within the septum): <20 is concerning for iron overload
	- Liver: <10 is concerning for iron overload
- Dr. Saghir likes to play the cine → if something turns black 🖤, then likely iron overload
